Developing a novel TP53 gene therapy to change the cancer treatment paradigm

Indel Bioinnovations is building a non-viral gene therapy designed to restore functional TP53 activity in cancer cells, beginning with triple-negative breast cancer and expanding across oncology.

Why TP53 matters
A central failure point in cancer biology
  • TP53 encodes p53, the “guardian of the genome”
  • Mutated in over 50% of cancers
  • Restoration may trigger apoptosis and cell-cycle arrest
  • Initial focus: TNBC with broader expansion potential

Science

Indel’s approach focuses on restoring tumor suppressor function rather than targeting downstream effects of cancer biology.

The problem

TNBC lacks targeted therapies and relies heavily on chemotherapy.

The solution

Reintroducing functional TP53 into cancer cells.

The outcome

Apoptosis, cell-cycle arrest, and restored checkpoints.
