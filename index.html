Why act now
The window for tumor suppressor restoration is opening

Cell and gene therapy has advanced materially in recent years, translational capabilities are stronger than ever, and the field now understands more about TP53 biology and genomic engineering opportunities than at any previous point. At the same time, TNBC remains an area of substantial unmet need.

Clinical urgency

TNBC is one of the most aggressive breast cancer subtypes and remains difficult to treat effectively.

Technology timing

Better genomic characterization and continued progress in gene engineering are creating new ways to pursue previously hard-to-address targets.

Development Timeline
